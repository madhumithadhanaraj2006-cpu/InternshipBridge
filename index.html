The Problem

Students often struggle to find suitable internships, track applications, and receive clear updates about their application status.

InternshipBridge provides a centralized platform where students can discover internships, submit applications, and track their application status.

Our Solution

A simple digital platform connecting students, colleges and companies.

🎓 Students

Discover internships and submit applications through one platform.

🏫 Institutions

Help students manage and track internship opportunities efficiently.

🏢 Companies

Connect with students and provide relevant internship opportunities.

How It Works

InternshipBridge follows a simple application process.

01. Login

Students create an account or login to the platform.

02. Find Internship

Explore available internship opportunities.

03. Apply

Submit your internship application.

04. Track

Track whether your application is Pending, Approved or Rejected.

Platform Features

Important features designed for simple internship management.

🔎 Internship Discovery

Students can explore available internship opportunities.

📝 Online Application

Students can submit internship applications online.

📊 Application Tracking

Students can check their application status.

✅ Admin Approval

Administrators can approve or reject applications.

🔐 Secure Login

Separate login access for administrators and students.

📋 Centralized Management

Internship applications are stored and managed through the system database.
